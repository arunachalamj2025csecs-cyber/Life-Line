About LifeLine

LifeLine Triage Assistant v1.0

A hybrid AI-powered pre-consultation triage system for rural primary health centers. Combines rule-based red-flag detection with LLM-driven conversational assessment.

Safety-First Design:

  • No diagnoses or prescriptions provided
  • All decisions overridable by doctors
  • Red-flag escalation for life-threatening conditions
  • Rationale provided for all classifications
